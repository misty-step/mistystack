# 2. Misty

Give your agent the outcome you want and the source material you already have. The installed [core](../../rules/core.md) is enough for small work: say what you will do, finish it, prove it on the real running path and keep it simple. For anything larger the core points to [misty](../../skills/misty/SKILL.md); you can also ask for it by name.

Misty is one page. It holds the work loop, who owns the code, what needs approval and what does not, when to delegate, how to report and where each task skill lives. It carries Misty Step's decisions and leaves general engineering knowledge to the model, so there is nothing to choose before asking for work. [ADR 0026](../decisions/0026-one-page-method.md) records why.

Projects keep their own `SPEC.md`, `DOMAIN.md`, decisions and verification path. [Foundation](../../skills/foundation/SKILL.md) checks the whole project against the [twelve articles](../../skills/foundation/checklist.md), rather than treating the touched file as the whole system.
