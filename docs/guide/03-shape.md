# 3. Shape

Two kinds of question come up before building. A question about what the product should do belongs to its owner: [grilling](../../skills/grilling/SKILL.md) and the other vendored shaping skills ask it well, and Misty's [shaping facts](../../skills/misty/shaping.md) direct settled outcomes straight into `SPEC.md` and `docs/decisions/`. A question about how something behaves is one the agent can answer by running it, so it runs a throwaway experiment instead of asking.

Keep the complete outcome in view. [Foundation article 3](../../skills/foundation/checklist.md#article-3) and [spec](../../skills/spec/SKILL.md) make one implementation-free `SPEC.md` the target state for each program, reconciled directly against the running product until they match rather than split into a ticket backlog.

Use [research](../../skills/research/SKILL.md) for unfamiliar contracts and [design](../../skills/design/SKILL.md) for interfaces. Use [agent-design](../../skills/agent-design/SKILL.md) when the deliverable instructs agents.
