# 3. Shape

Two kinds of question come up before building. A question about what the product should do belongs to its owner: [grilling](../../skills/grilling/SKILL.md) and the other vendored shaping skills ask it well, and Misty's [shaping facts](../../skills/misty/shaping.md) tell them where our tracker, domain terms and decisions live. A question about how something behaves is one the agent can answer by running it, so it runs a throwaway experiment instead of asking.

Keep the complete outcome in view. [Foundation article 3](../../skills/foundation/checklist.md#article-3) requires a living `SPEC.md`, not a particular skill for producing it. The [initiative story](../../USER_STORIES.md#us-053-lead-full-value-initiatives-through-atomic-delivery) owns slice acceptance.

Use [research](../../skills/research/SKILL.md) for unfamiliar contracts and [design](../../skills/design/SKILL.md) for interfaces. Use [agent-design](../../skills/agent-design/SKILL.md) when the deliverable instructs agents.
