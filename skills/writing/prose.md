# Misty prose rules

Apply Simplified Technical English as an editing discipline, not a claim of
certified ASD-STE100 compliance. Use a technical term when it is more exact than
an everyday substitute.

Treat about 20 words for an instruction and 25 for other sentences as inspection
points, not hard limits. Vary sentence length for clarity, not a manufactured rhythm.

Preserve the claim and its actual uncertainty while editing. An ordinary
technical use of a word is not a defect.

Prefer a useful sentence to a diagram. Use a diagram only when it makes the
relationships clearer. [Publications](publications.md) owns structural illustrations.
